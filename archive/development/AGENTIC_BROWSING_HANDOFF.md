## On-device tuning

The local operator uses 2,366 instruction characters versus 5,918 in the generic operator instructions (60% fewer), including the shopping follow-up below. Current page evidence is capped at 3,000 characters; recent action/page evidence gets a separate 2,000-character budget. Long-page `find_text` contributes up to 2,000 characters around the match. The compact-prompt regression reports 6,884 characters for a 40,000-character input fixture; these are character counts, not token counts or a general latency benchmark.

Literal “find … on this page” and “search this site/catalogue for …” instructions use deterministic planning once per task, then resume model reasoning from the observed result. Other choices use a dynamic FoundationModels schema. The same decoder, six-action guard, approval UI, exact-target binding and native executor still apply. A supporting quote must be present in supplied evidence before a local factual answer is accepted. Punctuation and small grammatical variations are tolerated, but dates and numbers in the answer must occur in that quote. A question asking when two events happened or comparing two explicitly named items must cover both before the answer is accepted. These deterministic checks target observed failure modes; they are not a general semantic fact checker. Ordinary answers and web-search summaries retain their existing paths.
